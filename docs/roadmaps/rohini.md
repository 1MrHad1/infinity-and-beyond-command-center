# Rohini Alam — 60-Day Roadmap
**Role:** Marketing / Brand & Content Lead
**Span:** 60 days · 165 tasks

## Weekly arc
| Week | Theme | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Foundation | Lock the agency's positioning, niche and ICP, stand up the marketing toolset, and agree team norms so brand work can begin. |
| 2 | Brand + Web Foundation | Ship the Infinity & Beyond brand identity and Canva brand kit, and write the core website copy and productized service definitions. |
| 3 | Website + Offer | Complete all site content and service pages, build the INR pricing narrative, and scaffold the portfolio with first sample work. |
| 4 | Content + Proof | Stand up the content engine (pillars, calendar, templates), publish first blog + case study, and batch two weeks of social posts. |
| 5 | Lead Generation | Launch the agency's own social channels as proof, ship a lead magnet, and wire up inbound + outbound lead-capture systems. |
| 6 | Outreach + Sales | Build the prospect list and sales collateral, run first outreach + follow-ups, and prepare proposals and discovery calls. |
| 7 | Delivery Systems | Document content and social SOPs, build the client onboarding kit, and deliver the first paying client's content batch. |
| 8 | Scale + Optimize | Read the analytics, refine offers and messaging, collect testimonials, activate referrals, and stand up a performance dashboard. |
| 9 | Launch Push + Review | Run the public launch campaign, analyze results, run a team retro, and write the next-quarter marketing plan. |
