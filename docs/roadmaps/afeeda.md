# Afeeda M.A — 60-Day Roadmap
**Role:** Junior Digital Marketer (Learning Track)
**Span:** 60 days · 178 tasks

## Weekly arc
| Week | Theme | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | WordPress + Elementor Foundations | Learn how websites and WordPress work, and build your first practice site in Elementor. |
| 2 | WordPress Mastery + Shopify Start | Finish a second polished WordPress site, then begin Shopify fundamentals. |
| 3 | Shopify Store Building + Meta Ads Intro | Complete a full demo Shopify store and start learning paid social on Meta. |
| 4 | Meta Ads & Paid Social | Learn to structure, target, and draft real Meta ad campaigns properly. |
| 5 | SEO + HTML/CSS Foundations | Master SEO fundamentals and gain real HTML/CSS coding skill to tweak sites. |
| 6 | Content, Canva, Email & Analytics | Create content and designs, build email flows, and read data in GA4 and Search Console. |
| 7 | Applying Skills to Agency Work | Shadow the team and ship your first real contributions to agency projects. |
| 8 | Owning Real Deliverables | Own a landing page, product setup, ad set, and email end-to-end with review. |
| 9 | Capstone & Growth Plan | Build a complete small-business site + funnel and plan your next 30 days. |

## Day-by-day

### Day 1 — Setup & the big picture
- **Map the digital marketing landscape** _(learn · ~90m)_ — Complete modules 1-3 of Google Digital Garage 'Fundamentals of Digital Marketing' and note where web dev, SEO, ads, and content each fit in an agency. [Google Digital Garage - Fundamentals of Digital Marketing](https://learndigital.withgoogle.com/digitalgarage)
- **Set up your learning workspace** _(admin · ~60m)_ — Create a Google Drive folder 'Afeeda-Learning' with a daily learning log doc, bookmark every course link, and share the log with Nishanth and Rohini.
- **Learn how websites and WordPress work** _(learn · ~90m)_ — Read WordPress.org's intro docs on domains, hosting, CMS, themes and plugins; write 5 clear notes explaining each in your own words. [WordPress.org Documentation](https://wordpress.org/documentation/)

### Day 2 — WordPress dashboard basics
- **Spin up a free WordPress sandbox** _(build · ~90m)_ — Launch a throwaway site in WordPress Playground (or a free InstaWP site) and explore the admin dashboard end to end. [WordPress Playground (free sandbox)](https://playground.wordpress.net/)
- **Take the WordPress beginner lessons** _(learn · ~90m)_ — Work through Learn WordPress beginner lessons on posts vs pages, media library, users and Settings. [Learn WordPress (official lessons)](https://learn.wordpress.org/)
- **Create your first pages and posts** _(practice · ~60m)_ — Add Home, About and Contact pages plus 2 blog posts with images, then set a static homepage. Checkpoint: paste screenshots into your log.

### Day 3 — Meet Elementor
- **Install Elementor and the Hello theme** _(learn · ~90m)_ — Follow Elementor Academy 'Getting Started' to install Elementor (free) and the Hello Elementor theme on your sandbox. [Elementor Academy](https://elementor.com/academy/)
- **Learn the Elementor editor** _(learn · ~90m)_ — Watch a beginner Elementor build video and learn sections, containers, columns, widgets and drag-and-drop. [Ferdy Korpershoek - Elementor tutorials](https://www.youtube.com/@FerdyKorpershoek)
- **Rebuild your homepage in Elementor** _(practice · ~90m)_ — Recreate your Home page hero and an about section using containers and widgets. Checkpoint: mobile preview looks clean.

### Day 4 — Layout & responsive design
- **Master containers, flexbox and spacing** _(learn · ~90m)_ — Study Elementor Academy lessons on flex containers, padding, margin and gap so layouts behave predictably. [Elementor Academy](https://elementor.com/academy/)
- **Build a responsive services section** _(practice · ~90m)_ — Create a 3-column services block that stacks neatly on mobile; test the tablet and mobile views.
- **Set global fonts and colors (brand kit)** _(learn · ~60m)_ — Configure global colors and typography and write 3 lines on why brand consistency matters. Checkpoint: 5-question self-quiz. [Elementor Academy](https://elementor.com/academy/)

### Day 5 — Practice Site 1 - Mysore cafe

- **Add a header and footer** _(practice · ~90m)_ — Create a simple nav header and a footer with contact details and an embedded map (note where Elementor Pro Theme Builder differs from free). [Elementor Academy](https://elementor.com/academy/)

### Day 6 — Forms & finishing Site 1

- **Finish and polish Practice Site 1** _(build · ~90m · ★ milestone)_ — Complete About and Contact, fix spacing, and verify mobile. Milestone: Practice Site 1 is complete.
